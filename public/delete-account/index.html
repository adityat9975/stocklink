Delete your StockLink account

You can delete your account yourself at any time. It takes under a minute.

Log in and delete my account

How to delete

  1. Tap Log in and delete my account above, or open the StockLink app.
  2. Log in with your phone number and password.
  3. Open the menu (your shop's letter at the top right) and choose Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password, type DELETE and confirm.

Finish or cancel any open orders first. Deletion cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your name, phone number, password and login sessions
  • Your notifications and phone alert subscriptions
  • Shops you own are closed: products hidden, invite codes stopped, connections ended
  • Your UPI ID

What is kept, and why

  • Past orders, because the other shop needs them for its bills and GST records. They show as "Deleted user".
  • A security log entry saying the account was deleted, with no personal details.

Cannot log in? Reset access with the shop that invited you, or contact StockLink support from the app's website.
