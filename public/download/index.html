How it works

For wholesalers

  1. Sign up with your GST number and add your products with photos and rates.
  2. Share your shop code with your retailers on WhatsApp.
  3. Approve who can see your stock. Orders arrive live: confirm, pack, hand over.

For retailers

  1. Install StockLink and register your shop. It's free.
  2. Enter your wholesaler's shop code. Once they approve, you see their live stock and your rate.
  3. Order, pay and collect. Chat with them right inside the order.

Questions

Is it free?

Retailers use StockLink free, always. Wholesalers pay a small monthly plan.

Can anyone see my prices?

No. Only retailers you approve can see your stock, and each one sees only their own rate. You can block a retailer any time.

Do I need the Play Store?

No. StockLink installs straight from this website on Android and iPhone, takes less than 1 MB and updates itself.

I have thousands of products. Do I have to add all of them?

No. Start with the 20–50 items your retailers ask about most. Add more when you want.

How do payments work?

You choose what to accept: UPI to your own UPI ID, cash at pickup, cash on delivery or credit with a limit per retailer. Money goes straight to you. StockLink never holds it.
