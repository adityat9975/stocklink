STOCKLINK: 3 REELS A DAY, FIRST 7 DAYS
Post at about 9 am, 2 pm and 8 pm. Each reel is 7–15 seconds, vertical, phone-shot is fine.
Add a trending Instagram sound inside the app (the 3 ready reels have no music on purpose).
Every reel ends with: "Link in bio → StockLink install karein".
Keep text away from the bottom 20% of the screen; Instagram's buttons cover it.

DAY 1 (8 Nov, launch)
1. READY: reel1_calls_problem.mp4. Caption: "Roz 50 calls? Ab nahi."
2. READY: reel2_order_30sec.mp4. Caption: "30 second mein order. Seriously."
3. READY: reel3_wholesaler_private.mp4. Caption: "Aapka stock, sirf aapke retailers ko."

DAY 2
1. Shop counter: phone rings again and again; owner looks tired. Text: "Har call = 2 minute waste". Cut to StockLink on screen.
2. Screen recording: wholesaler adds one product with a photo in 20 sec. Text: "Product add karna = 20 second".
3. Text on red: "Retailer ke liye kitne ka hai? ₹0. Hamesha." with the app logo.

DAY 3
1. POV retailer at 10 pm: "Kal ka maal order karna hai, shop band hai". Opens StockLink, orders. Text: "Raat ko bhi order".
2. Split screen: WhatsApp list (messy) vs StockLink catalog (clean). Text: "Ye vs Ye".
3. Screen recording: pickup code on the retailer phone, wholesaler checks it at the counter. Text: "No confusion. Code dikhao, maal lo."

DAY 4
1. Myth vs fact: "Myth: sab log mera rate dekh lenge. Fact: sirf approved retailer, apna rate."
2. Screen recording: Chat on an order: "Ready?" → "Haan, 4 baje". Text: "Order ke saath chat".
3. Real wholesaler (pilot shop) 10-second review in Hindi. Ask permission and use their real words only.

DAY 5
1. Screen recording: cash and delivery switches in Shop settings. Text: "Cash lena hai ya nahi? Aap decide karo."
2. Countdown "3 cheezein jo StockLink se band ho jaati hain: 1. Rate ke calls 2. Galat order 3. Udhaar ka hisaab bhoolna"
3. Install guide: open the website → Install → home screen icon. Text: "Play Store nahi chahiye".

DAY 6
1. "Ek din ki kahani": morning orders arriving (notifications), afternoon packing, evening payment received.
2. Retailer reorders usual items in one tap. Text: "Wahi maal, 1 tap mein".
3. Question reel: "Aap roz kitne calls lete ho stock ke liye? Comment karo 👇" (boosts comments).

DAY 7
1. Week 1 numbers from your pilot (only real numbers): "Week 1: __ shops, __ orders".
2. Screen recording: "Item back in stock" alert on a retailer phone. Text: "Maal aate hi retailer ko pata".
3. Remix the best Day 1–6 reel with a new hook in the first 1 second.

RULES THAT DECIDE IF A REEL WORKS
- First 1 second: show the problem (ringing phone, messy list), not the logo.
- Big Hinglish text, 5–7 words per screen.
- Never show fake numbers, fake reviews or other shops' real prices.
- Check Insights after 48 hours; make more of whatever got the most shares and saves.
